🇮🇳 Bharat Chronicle

Your Daily Window to India

Bharat Chronicle is a modern, multilingual Indian digital news platform designed to bring news from across India into one convenient destination.

The platform collects news headlines from RSS feeds, organizes them into categories and regions, and aims to use Google Gemini AI to generate original news summaries in English, Tamil and Hindi.

---

🌟 Project Overview

Bharat Chronicle aims to make Indian news accessible, organized and easy to understand.

Key Features

- 📰 News Aggregation – Collect news from configured RSS feeds.
- 🇮🇳 National News – Latest news from across India.
- 🗺️ Regional News – State-wise and regional news coverage.
- 🤖 AI News Summaries – Gemini-powered original news summaries.
- 🌐 Multilingual Support – English, Tamil and Hindi.
- 🔍 Smart Search – Find news by keywords and categories.
- 🔖 Bookmarks – Save interesting news stories.
- 🚨 Breaking News – Highlight important news updates.
- 📱 Responsive Design – Optimized for mobile, tablet and desktop.
- 📡 RSS Integration – Collect updates from configured news sources.

🤖 AI Integration

Bharat Chronicle plans to use Google's Gemini API for:

- News summarization
- Multilingual translation
- News categorization
- Headline generation
- Regional news classification

AI-generated content should be reviewed by an administrator before publication.

AI must not invent facts, quotes or statistics. News summaries should retain source attribution and links to original reporting.

🚀 Deployment

The intended deployment environment is Google Cloud Run.

General deployment workflow:

1. Develop and test the application in Google AI Studio.
2. Configure environment variables and API credentials.
3. Connect the project to Google Cloud.
4. Deploy the application to Cloud Run.
5. Configure a custom domain.
6. Test the live website.

🔐 Environment Variables

Keep API keys and credentials on the server.

Example configuration:

GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key
GOOGLE_CLOUD_PROJECT=your_project_id

Do not commit real API keys, passwords or service account credentials to GitHub.

Use the actual environment variable names required by the application.

📰 Editorial Standards

Bharat Chronicle aims to provide responsible digital news coverage.

- Attribute news to its original publisher.
- Respect copyright and licensing restrictions.
- Avoid publishing unverified information.
- Distinguish allegations from confirmed facts.
- Review AI-generated summaries before publication.
- Correct factual errors when identified.
- Provide links to original reporting.

RSS availability does not automatically grant permission to republish full articles or images.
